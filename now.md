*Updated November 2026. This is a [now page](https://nownownow.com/about), which lists what I'm focused on this month.*

**At work.** I lead a team of seven data and AI engineers at a 3,500-person global manufacturer. Most of my attention right now is on governing the enterprise data lake my team and I have been building. In practice, that means ownership, quality standards, and access policy for what's in the lake, and the ongoing work of keeping all three current. I'm doing the same for the AI systems the team now ships.

**Writing.** I'm publishing the [field notes](/field-notes/), retrospectives on the platform, governance, cost, and leadership work of the last decade, each labeled with the period it covers. The first one up is on causal business models. The next are on finance-grade reporting on the close calendar and on business equations.

**Thinking about.** Advocating for businesses to move faster on organizational updates, so they can make the most of new tools and technology in the AI era. The tech has changed dramatically. If your org chart hasn't changed at all in the last year, are you suggesting that, in the face of the most exciting and transformative technology in decades, your org chart is perfect enough to stay as-is? I'd encourage leaders to think about what new roles and relationships they could be building to better align with the modern tech landscape.

**Looking.** I'm looking for a senior role leading finance or business analytics, where the job is turning the questions Finance and the business are asking into data products, models, and AI tools, and building the team that maintains them. Remote or in Colorado Springs. If you're hiring for a role like that, [email me](mailto:kyle.ries@gmail.com).

**On my own time.** I'm tinkering with home energy data (EV consumption profiling from interval-meter data) and riding bikes in the Front Range.
